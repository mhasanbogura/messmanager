## Overview

A simple mess management application designed to help users organize shared-mess information, track members, meals, expenses, and monthly calculations in one place.

## Features

- Manage mess members
- Track daily meals
- Manage meal rates and meal counts
- Record shared expenses
- Track individual member expenses
- Calculate monthly meal costs
- Manage deposits and balances
- View monthly summaries
- Track mess-related transactions
- Simple and organized interface

## Permissions

- **Storage access** — Save and access application data when required
- **Internet access** — Support online features or synchronization when available
- **Notifications** — Display reminders, updates, or mess-related information

## Best for

Students, roommates, and shared-mess residents who want an organized way to manage members, meals, expenses, deposits, and monthly mess calculations.
